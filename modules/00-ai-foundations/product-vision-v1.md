# 🌱 AI Plant Doctor – Product Vision v1

## 📌 Product Overview

**AI Plant Doctor** is an AI-powered assistant designed to help small-scale crop farmers in Nigeria identify plant diseases and nutrient deficiencies early. Farmers can describe plant symptoms or upload images of affected crops to receive likely diagnoses, treatment recommendations, and preventive advice.

---

## 🎯 Target Users

The primary users of this product are:

- Small-scale crop farmers in Nigeria
- Farmers growing crops such as:
  - Maize
  - Cassava
  - Rice
  - Tomatoes
  - Vegetables
  - Yam
  - Pepper
  - Other arable crops

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Small-scale crop farmers often struggle to identify plant diseases and nutrient deficiencies early enough, leading to reduced yields and financial losses.

Many farmers also have limited access to agricultural experts, especially in rural communities, making it difficult to receive timely and reliable advice.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

Build an AI-powered assistant that enables farmers to:

- Upload a picture of an affected plant.
- Describe the symptoms they observe.
- Receive a likely diagnosis.
- Get treatment recommendations.
- Learn preventive measures to reduce future outbreaks.

The goal is to provide farmers with fast, accessible, and reliable agricultural guidance anytime they need it.

---

## ⭐ Core Features (MVP)

Version 1 of the product will focus on solving **one problem well**.

### Feature 1
📷 Upload a plant image.

### Feature 2
✍️ Describe plant symptoms using text.

### Feature 3
🤖 AI-generated diagnosis of possible diseases or nutrient deficiencies.

### Feature 4
💊 Treatment recommendations.

### Feature 5
🌿 Preventive tips to avoid future occurrences.

---

## 📊 Success Metrics

The MVP will be considered successful if it achieves the following:

- 1,000 registered users.
- 500 monthly active users.
- 80% of users report that the AI recommendations were helpful.
- Average AI response time of less than 5 seconds.
- Average user rating of at least **4.5/5**.

---

## 🤖 Why AI?

Artificial Intelligence is essential because it can:

- Analyze both text descriptions and uploaded images.
- Recognize disease patterns.
- Generate personalized recommendations.
- Respond much faster than waiting to consult an agricultural expert.
- Improve over time as more agricultural knowledge becomes available.

Without AI, farmers would rely only on static information that may not fit their specific situation.
