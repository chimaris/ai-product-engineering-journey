# Lesson 1 - What is AI Product Engineering?

## Key Takeaways

- AI Product Engineering is about building AI-powered products that solve real user problems.
- AI is only one component of a complete product.
- Successful AI products combine:
  - Software Engineering
  - AI Engineering
  - Product Management
  - UX Design
  - Business Strategy

## Important Principle

> People don't pay for AI.
> They pay for problems solved.

## Difference Between Roles

### AI Engineer

Focuses on:

- Building AI capabilities
- Model performance
- AI infrastructure
- Evaluation
- Optimization

### AI Product Engineer

Focuses on:

- User problems
- Product value
- AI integration
- User experience
- Business outcomes

2. Research
3. Product Strategy
4. UX Design
5. Architecture
6. Development
7. AI Evaluation
8. Testing
9. Deployment
10. Monitoring
11. Iteration

## Important Principle

Never start with technology.

Always start with the user's problem.

## Our Product

### Working Title

AI Plant Doctor

### Target Users

Small-scale crop farmers in Nigeria.

### MVP

Allow farmers to:

- Upload plant images
- Describe symptoms
- Receive AI diagnosis
- Get treatment recommendations
- Learn preventive measures

## Product Success Metrics

- 1,000 registered users
- 500 Monthly Active Users
- 80% positive feedback
- Response time under 5 seconds
- User rating ≥ 4.5/5

# Lesson 3 - Why Python Dominates AI

## Python dominates AI because of:

1. Simple syntax
2. Rich AI ecosystem
3. AI research is Python-first
4. Huge developer community
5. Mature AI frameworks

## Important AI Frameworks

- NumPy
- pandas
- scikit-learn
- PyTorch
- TensorFlow
- FastAPI
- LangChain
- LlamaIndex
- CrewAI
- LangGraph
